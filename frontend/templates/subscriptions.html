Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ₹1,000 limit work in the Normal Pack? ▾
With the Normal Pack, any live event whose registration or pass price is up to ₹1,000 is 100% free to watch. If an exclusive or celebrity event costs more than ₹1,000 (e.g. ₹1,200 or ₹1,500), Normal Pack subscribers can either purchase a single pass for that event or upgrade to the Super Pack for unlimited access to all events!
Can I stream in 1080p or 2K on the Normal Pack? ▾
The Normal Pack is capped at 720p HD. If you want ultra-crisp 1080p Full HD or 2K QHD video quality, you will need the Super Pack.
How many devices can watch at the same time? ▾
Just like Hotstar Mobile / Super plans, both Normal and Super packs support 1 active streaming session at a time per account. If playback is started on a second device, you will be prompted to watch on the new device.
Can I cancel or upgrade my subscription anytime? ▾
Yes! You can upgrade from Normal Pack to Super Pack anytime with pro-rated billing, or cancel auto-renewal with 1 click while retaining access until your billing cycle concludes.
